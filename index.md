I am an Assistant Professor at SANKEN, The University of Osaka. My research focuses on **data mining and machine learning for complex, evolving data**.

I develop scalable and interpretable methods for data streams, time series, tensors, and dynamic networks, with applications including anomaly detection, event analysis, and forecasting.

[Research]({{ '/research.html' | relative_url }}) · [About and contact]({{ '/about.html#contact' | relative_url }}) · [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?view_op=search_authors&mauthors=Koki+Kawabata) · [GitHub](https://github.com/kokikwbt) · [ORCID](https://orcid.org/0000-0003-4202-4513)

## Research interests

- **Data streams and time series.** Online modeling, change detection, and forecasting for continuously evolving data.
- **Tensor and network mining.** Scalable methods for multi-aspect data and dynamic interaction structures.
- **Anomaly detection.** Interpretable detection of unusual behavior in complex real-world systems.
